# R19 — Muse response

The standard iPhone 16 has the longer battery life on Apple's video playback estimate.

- iPhone 15: Video playback up to 20 hours, streamed up to 16 hours.
- iPhone 16: Video playback up to 22 hours, streamed up to 18 hours.

So the iPhone 16 is 2 hours longer for video playback, and 2 hours longer for streamed video playback. Sources:

- [iPhone 15 - Tech Specs, Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111831)
- [iPhone 16 - Tech Specs, Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121029)
