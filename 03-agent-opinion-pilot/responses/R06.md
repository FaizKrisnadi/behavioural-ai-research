# R06 — OpenBot response

**Conclusion: the iPhone 16 has the longer battery life** by Apple's stated video playback estimates — about 2 hours more than the iPhone 15, both for local and streamed playback. Apple's official numbers (standard models):

| Metric | iPhone 15 | iPhone 16 |
| Video playback (local) | Up to 20 hours | Up to 22 hours |
| Video playback (streamed) | Up to 16 hours | Up to 18 hours |
| Audio playback | Up to 80 hours | Up to 80 hours |

Evidence and sources:

- iPhone 16 — Apple's live technical specifications page (https://www.apple.com/iphone-16/specs/) lists "Up to 22 hours" video playback and "Up to 18 hours" streamed, under Power and Battery.
- iPhone 15 — Apple has since retired the iPhone 15 specs URL (it now redirects to the current lineup), so I verified the number from an archived copy of Apple's official "iPhone 15 Technical Specifications" page (Wayback Machine snapshot, Oct 2, 2024, at https://web.archive.org/web/20241002150924/https://www.apple.com/iphone-15/specs/), which lists "Up to 20 hours" video playback and "Up to 16 hours" streamed for the standard iPhone 15.
- Cross-check: Apple's compare page (https://www.apple.com/iphone/compare/) is the canonical source of the same figures.

One caveat: these are Apple's own test estimates (repeated HDR movie playback, default settings), so real-world battery life varies with usage. No purchase made — let me know if you want a deeper comparison of the two models.
