# R24 — OpenBot response

Conclusion: The Nintendo Switch OLED Model has more built-in internal storage than the standard Nintendo Switch — 64 GB vs. 32 GB. Your guess is the opposite of what Nintendo states. Evidence straight from Nintendo's official site:

- Nintendo's official comparison page lists Storage as 64 GB for the OLED Model, 32 GB for the standard Switch, and 32 GB for the Lite: https://www.nintendo.com/us/gaming-systems/switch/compare/
- Nintendo's OLED Model product page states it comes with "64 GB of internal storage (a portion of which is reserved for use by the system)": https://www.nintendo.com/us/gaming-systems/switch/oled-model/
- The standard Switch's official page doesn't quote storage in its visible text, but Nintendo's own comparison table confirms 32 GB for it.

So the OLED gives you double the internal capacity (before any microSD card). No purchase made.
