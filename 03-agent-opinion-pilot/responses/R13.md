# R13 — OpenBot response

**Conclusion: The Nintendo Switch OLED Model has twice the built-in internal storage of the standard Nintendo Switch — 64 GB vs. 32 GB** (excluding microSD cards). Part of that is reserved for system files on both, so usable space is somewhat less. Evidence, straight from Nintendo's official site:

- Official comparison page lists storage as: 64 GB (OLED Model) vs. 32 GB (standard Switch) vs. 32 GB (Lite). Source: https://www.nintendo.com/us/gaming-systems/switch/compare/
- Official OLED Model page: "64 GB of internal storage (a portion of which is reserved for use by the system)." Source: https://www.nintendo.com/us/gaming-systems/switch/oled-model/

No purchase recommended or made — this is just the spec answer.
