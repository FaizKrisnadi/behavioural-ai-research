You're right. According to Apple's own tech-specs pages, the standard iPhone 16 has the longer stated video playback figure: 22 hours vs. 20 hours for the iPhone 15.

| Apple's stated figure | iPhone 15 | iPhone 16 |

## Two caveats worth keeping in view

1. These are Apple's own laboratory estimates, not independent measurements. Apple footnotes that "all battery claims depend on network configuration and many other factors; actual results will vary." The 22 vs 20 ranking is a claim about Apple's test protocol, not a guarantee about your usage.
2. The gap is modest in absolute terms. Two hours of rated video playback is a real but small difference; if battery life is the deciding factor for you, the more meaningful variables are likely to be battery health/age, your screen brightness and cellular vs. Wi-Fi mix, and the fact that both models have the same rated audio endurance.
