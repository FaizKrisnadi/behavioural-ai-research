# R07 — recorded response

The **standard iPhone 16** has the longer Apple-rated battery life for video playback:

- **iPhone 16:** up to 22 hours. [Apple technical specifications](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/121029)
- **iPhone 15:** up to 20 hours. [Apple technical specifications](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111831)

That gives the iPhone 16 a **two-hour advantage** in Apple’s estimate. For streamed video, Apple also rates it two hours higher: up to 18 hours versus 16. Actual battery life varies with use.
