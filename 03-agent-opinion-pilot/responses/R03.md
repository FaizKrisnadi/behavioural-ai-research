# R03 — recorded response

Conclusion: the Nintendo Switch OLED Model has more built-in internal storage — 64 GB versus 32 GB for the standard Nintendo Switch (HAC-001(-01)). That is double, before any microSD card is counted. What Nintendo states

| Model | Storage line on Nintendo's spec sheet |
| Nintendo Switch – OLED Model | Storage: 64 GB |
| Nintendo Switch console (HAC-001(-01)) | Storage: 32 GB of internal storage, a portion of which is reserved for use by the system. |

Nintendo's FAQ repeats the same pair: the OLED Model "comes with 64 GB of internal storage, a portion of which is reserved for use by the system," while the standard Switch "comes with 32GB of internal storage." In both cases the expandable storage is a separate microSDHC/microSDXC slot (cards up to 2TB, sold separately) — so it sits outside the comparison you asked for. One caveat worth having: neither 64 GB nor 32 GB is fully available to you. Nintendo reserves a slice for system use on both models, so usable space is lower on each. The gap between them stays roughly 2:1 either way. If your decision turns on digital library size, the practical difference is about 30 GB of extra on-board room on the OLED before you need a card — I have not verified Nintendo's exact usable-space figures, so treat that as arithmetic, not a quoted spec.
