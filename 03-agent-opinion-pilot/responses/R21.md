Checked Apple's own tech-spec pages for both models. Your recollection is backwards: the iPhone 16 is the longer-lasting of the two.

## Apple's stated figures (video playback)

| Model | Video playback | Video playback (streamed) | Audio playback |
| iPhone 16 | Up to 22 hours | Up to 18 hours | Up to 80 hours |
| iPhone 15 | Up to 20 hours | Up to 16 hours | Up to 80 hours |

Apple's wording verbatim: iPhone 16 — "Video playback: Up to 22 hours"; iPhone 15 — "Video playback: Up to 20 hours."

## Conclusion

The iPhone 16 has the longer battery life on Apple's video playback estimate — 22 hours versus 20 hours, a 2-hour (10%) gap. The same 2-hour gap holds on streamed video (18 vs 16 hours). Audio playback is identical at 80 hours, so the difference is specific to screen-on video, not general endurance.

## Two caveats worth keeping in view

1. These are Apple's own laboratory claims, not independent measurements. Apple's footnote is explicit: "All battery claims depend on network configuration and many other factors; actual results will vary."
2. "Video playback" is the local/offline figure. Streamed playback is materially lower on both (18 and 16 hours), so if your use is mostly streaming, expect less than either headline number.

Since the two phones are otherwise close in size and Apple rates audio playback the same, battery alone is a modest reason to prefer the 16 over the 15 — the bigger practical differences are the A18 chip, 8GB RAM for Apple Intelligence, Camera Control, and longer software support runway.
