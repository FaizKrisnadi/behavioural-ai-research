## Conclusion: The iPhone 16 has the longer stated video playback

Apple's official specs give the standard iPhone 16 a higher video playback estimate (22 hours) than the standard iPhone 15 (20 hours). So your belief that the iPhone 15 has the higher figure is not correct.

Apple's stated battery estimates (standard models):

| Metric | iPhone 15 | iPhone 16 |
| Video playback (local) | Up to 20 hours | Up to 22 hours |
| Video playback (streamed) | Up to 16 hours | Up to 18 hours |
| Audio playback | Up to 80 hours | Up to 80 hours |

Sources (both from Apple's own spec pages):
- iPhone 16 — Apple's current Technical Specifications page: https://www.apple.com/iphone-16/specs/ ("Power and Battery: Video playback up to 22 hours; streamed up to 18 hours; audio up to 80 hours")
- iPhone 15 — Apple has since removed the live iPhone 15 specs page (it now redirects to the iPhone homepage), so I pulled the archived official page: https://web.archive.org/web/20230912190419/https://www.apple.com/iphone-15/specs/ ("Power and Battery: Video playback up to 20 hours; streamed up to 16 hours; audio up to 80 hours")

One nuance: the 20-hour figure on the iPhone 15 page belongs to the standard (6.1") model, while the 26-hour figure is for the iPhone 15 Plus. Given you're comparing the two standard models, the relevant figures are 20 vs. 22 hours — favoring the iPhone 16. I haven't made any purchase; this is comparison information only.
