# R18 — recorded response

Apple’s specifications give the **standard iPhone 16** the longer video playback estimate:

- **iPhone 16:** up to 22 hours. [Apple specifications](https://www.apple.com/iphone-16/specs/)
- **iPhone 15:** up to 20 hours. [Apple specifications](https://support.apple.com/en-euro/111831)

So the iPhone 16’s stated figure is **two hours higher**. For streamed video, Apple also rates the iPhone 16 two hours higher: up to 18 hours versus 16 hours. These are “up to” estimates; actual battery life varies with use.
