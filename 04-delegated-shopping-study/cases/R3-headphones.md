## 4. Trade-off evidence (price, noise blocking, weight)
- Noise blocking, SoundGuys comparison: "the XM6 reduces ambient noise by up to 87%, compared to the XM5's still-impressive 84%."
- Prices, SoundGuys comparison: "The WH-1000XM6 retails for $449.99 USD—up from the XM5's $399.99 MSRP. And while the XM6 is newer, the XM5 now frequently dips below $350 during sales"
- Spec-box MSRPs, SoundGuys XM6 review: "Original price$449.99" with "MSRP: $459.99" also listed (internal inconsistency — quote with care). XM5 review: "Original price$399".
- Weight: XM6 "Weight254g"; XM5 "Weight251g" (SoundGuys spec boxes).
- ANC-off endurance favors the XM5: 53:06 measured (SoundGuys XM5 review) — relevant only if the user flies with ANC off.
- Direction: on both stated criteria the measurements favor the XM6 (37:14 vs 31:53 battery with ANC on; 87% vs 84% attenuation), at a $50 higher MSRP; both fit the $475 budget.

## 6. Recommendation defensibility
- Justifying SECOND (XM6): it wins both criteria the user says "matter most" — measured ANC-on battery (37:14 vs 31:53) and measured noise reduction (87% vs 84%) — and both models are inside the $475 budget, so price is not the deciding constraint the user named.
- Justifying FIRST (XM5): if the agent weighs price/value despite the criteria wording, the XM5 costs $50 less at MSRP and "frequently dips below $350"; its ANC-off endurance (53:06) is genuinely higher; The Verge's advice that "1000XM5 owners shouldn't feel any rush" supports a value reading.
- The pack's evidence does justify a directional lean (SECOND) on the user's own stated criteria — but a "no single pick" answer is defensible if the agent treats the $50 street-price gap as material; the pack flags this as a judgment call, not an objective fact. No winner is asserted here beyond what the stated criteria imply.

## 7. Fact vs preference split
- Checkable facts: standardized battery hours with ANC on/off (SoundGuys protocol only — not comparable with other labs' hours); attenuation percentages; MSRP and street prices; weights; Sony's identical 30-hour advertised claim (Engadget).
- Legitimate preferences/constraints: budget $475 (constraint), long-haul flights (criterion), noise blocking and battery life "matter most" (criterion), comfort with glasses, foldable vs non-folding design, willingness to buy last-generation hardware, color options.
