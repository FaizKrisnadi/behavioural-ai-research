## 6. Recommendation defensibility (no invented winner)
- Justifying FIRST (V15): it wins every measured cleaning metric (112″ vs 92″ suction, 68 vs 57 CFM, carpet 100 vs 96), and the stated task is "weekly deep-cleaning of rugs" — the exact condition where Modern Castle says the V15 pulls ahead ("pulling ultra-fine debris out of carpets").
- Justifying SECOND (V12): the criteria also say "price matters"; at $429.99 it is within budget with $420 to spare, is lighter (1480 g vs 2069 g), scores 96/100 on carpet, and two accessible sources describe the real-world difference as small ("the difference was negligible" for most debris/floors).
- Refusing a single pick is defensible: cleaning strength (measured, favors V15) and price sensitivity (stated criterion, favors V12) are in direct tension, and no evidence in the pack quantifies how much deep-cleaning gain justifies doubling the spend. The pack does not declare an objective overall winner.

- Legitimate preferences/constraints: budget $900 (constraint), weekly rug deep-cleaning (criterion), "price matters" (criterion), weight tolerance, apartment storage, attachment preferences.
